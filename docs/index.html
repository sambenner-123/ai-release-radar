    How the numbers work. For each model, every open “released by …?” and daily “released on …?” market is turned into a point on one release-date curve (daily buckets are cumulated and normalised). A volume-weighted isotonic fit makes the curve non-decreasing, and between market deadlines the chance is interpolated at a constant hazard rate. The 7- and 30-day figures are read off that curve; “50/50 by” is where it crosses 50%.

    “By lab” combines a lab’s models assuming they’re independent, so treat it as rough. “Thin” marks models with under $2k traded across their open markets. Prices are Polymarket’s displayed probabilities, not advice.
